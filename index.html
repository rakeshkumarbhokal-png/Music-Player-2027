1. Introduction

Welcome to Music Player 2027 ("we", "our", or "us"). We highly respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data and device security of every user.

This Privacy Policy explains how Music Player 2027 handles information when you install and use our Android mobile application. Music Player 2027 is fundamentally an offline-first local music player designed to play songs and audio files stored directly on your mobile device.

2. Information We Collect and Access

We believe in data minimization. The application does not collect, record, or transmit personal details such as your name, phone number, physical address, contacts, or financial information.

The information accessed by the app is limited to what is strictly necessary to provide the core media playback experience:

  • Local Media & Audio Files: The app accesses audio files (such as MP3, M4A, FLAC, AAC, WAV) and related audio tags (artist, album, title, album cover art) stored on your device storage to index them into playlists, albums, and song lists. This metadata is stored locally on your device in a secure private database and is never uploaded to any remote server.
  • Playback State & Preferences: User preferences, equalizer presets, volume balance, playlists, favorite songs, sleep timer settings, and cutter clip configurations are stored strictly within the app's local sandbox on your device.
  • Non-Personal Device & Diagnostics Information: Standard technical information may be collected automatically by Google Play Services or advertising SDKs (e.g., device model, operating system version, anonymous advertising identifiers) for crash diagnosis, performance monitoring, and ad serving.

3. Android Permissions & Why We Need Them

To provide audio playback, equalizer features, and lock-screen controls, the application requests the following Android permissions:

Permission Purpose & Rationale
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO
(Android 13+)		 Required to read and display audio files stored in your device storage so you can browse, search, and play your music.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
(Android 12 & below)		 Used on older Android versions strictly to access local music tracks stored on the internal storage or SD card.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE &
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK		 Required by Android to keep playback playing seamlessly when the app is minimized, when you switch to other apps, or when your screen is locked.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays media playback controls (Play, Pause, Skip Next, Previous, Song Title, Album Art) in your system notification shade and lock screen.
RECORD_AUDIO Used strictly for the real-time audio visualizer waveform and equalizer animation during active playback. The app does not record, save, or transmit any microphone speech or ambient sounds.
MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS &
WAKE_LOCK		 Used to configure the 15-band multi-effect equalizer, hardware bass boost, and ensure audio does not stutter when device enters sleep mode.
VIBRATE Provides subtle tactile haptic feedback during interactive controls such as scrubbing, cutting music clips, or adjusting equalizer sliders.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support free development and maintenance of the application, Music Player 2027 may display third-party advertisements via Google AdMob and Google Play Services.

These third-party service providers may use standard mobile identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID - AAID) and cookies to serve contextual or personalized advertisements in accordance with Google Play policies:

  • Google Play Services: Core Android system services for application stability and license verification. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: For displaying mobile banner, interstitial, or native ads. How Google uses information from sites or apps

You can manage or reset your advertising identifier, or opt-out of personalized advertisements at any time through your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID / Reset advertising ID.

5. Audio Trimming & Tag Editing (Music Cutter)

When using the built-in Music Cutter or Ringtone Maker, trimmed audio files are saved locally into your device's audio storage directory. Music Player 2027 will never delete or overwrite your original source audio files without explicit user interaction and confirmation.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Music Player 2027 does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Our application is a general-audience local media utility. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at our developer email address, and we will promptly take corrective action.

7. Data Security & Storage

Because all music indexing, audio decoding, and playback happen locally on your smartphone or tablet, your private media library never leaves your device. We do not maintain any cloud database of your personal files or listening history.

Uninstalling the application or clearing app storage in device settings removes all locally saved playlists, equalizer presets, and app cache immediately.

8. Your Rights & Choices (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your geographic location (including the European Economic Area, UK, and California), you have rights regarding your data:

  • Access and Portability: You maintain complete control over your music files; they remain in your standard device directories.
  • Revoking Permissions: You can grant or revoke any app permission (Storage, Microphone/Audio, Notifications) at any time through Android Settings > Apps > Music Player 2027 > Permissions.
  • Opting Out of Tracking: You can opt-out of personalized advertising via Google device settings.
